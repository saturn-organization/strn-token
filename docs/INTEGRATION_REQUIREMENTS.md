# External integration requirements

These requirements apply to future governance-platform, points/distributor and product-fee integrations. Those systems are not implemented in this repository and are excluded from the STRN+sSTRN production audit scope. The tokens expose voting checkpoints, recovery quantities, position events and a discount-percentage getter. Implementations of the external systems require their own verification and review.

## Governance platform

At one past timestamp t, a governance strategy sums STRN.getPastVotes(delegate,t) and StakedSTRN.getPastVotes(delegate,t). STRN already excludes custody backing from delegated weight, so do not subtract it a second time. Staking exchanges liquid units for receipt units; redemption reverses this. Both token total-supply checkpoints retain recovered balances. For a full-fixed-supply denominator under the recovery exclusion policy, begin with STRN.getPastTotalSupply(t) and subtract STRN.getPastRecoveredPrincipal(t) plus StakedSTRN.getPastRecoveredPrincipal(t). Never add sSTRN total supply to STRN total supply. Further circulating-supply/treasury exclusions require their own specified historical strategy. This repository provides the query primitives, not a Governor or deployed platform strategy. Delegation/snapshot choices can leave part of supply non-voting without burning it.

Historical proposal snapshots are not rewritten by later seizure or blacklist changes. Emergency cancellation or retrospective disqualification requires a separately specified governance mechanism. Recovery exclusions can lower future quorum thresholds; that consequence belongs to platform policy, not an onchain supply burn.

## Points treatment after seizure

Neither token stores or pays points. Distinguish rescue from enforcement without changing principal backing or original maturities.

- Pending classification: suspend affected-account points accrual and payout; retain historical records and the otherwise eligible accrual needed to resolve a rescue. Blacklisting or a seizure event alone does not establish misconduct.
- Verified rescue: preserve accrued unpaid points and transfer entitlement to the verified replacement address. Restore otherwise eligible accrual during rescue custody, bounded by each position's original maturity and the applicable season rules. Do not extend a lock or award points after maturity merely because recovery continues. Preserve the original beneficiary history and prevent a second claim by the compromised address.
- Confirmed enforcement: cancel the affected account's entire unpaid points balance across positions and points sources in this program, including unpaid prior-season balances. This is account eligibility treatment, not a fraction calculated from tokens seized; partial seizure still has the same cancellation scope. Stop further accrual from the seizure cutoff, including on unseized balances. Do not automatically extend cancellation to unrelated accounts or programs. Cancellation covers points and unpaid allocations derived from those points, not principal or unrelated financial claims.
- Release of tokens does not itself reverse an enforcement cancellation or reinstate account points eligibility. Any later reinstatement requires an explicit recorded points-policy decision; it does not automatically restore cancelled balances.

Link each decision to an auditable case record containing chain ID, contract, transaction hash and log index, affected account, position IDs where applicable, classification/status, effective cutoff, policy version, authorized decision and verified rescue recipient. Keep identity evidence private, referenced by the case rather than published onchain or in this repository. Classify cases explicitly; do not infer rescue from a transfer destination or enforcement from blacklist status. Points eligibility/classification authority is an operational permission, not a new token role or a power inferred from SEIZER_ROLE. Seizure and release remain immediate contract operations.

Events identify prior owner, custody, recipient and whether release paid underlying, but contain no reason classification. For liquid STRN, link the Seized event and subsequent rescue delivery to the case; an ordinary recovery-wallet transfer alone is insufficient evidence to migrate points. Retain historical records after live position deletion. Already distributed tokens are not clawed back by cancelling points. No renewal, fee discounts or votes are restored during recovery by preserving rescue points. No points service, case-management system or distributor enforcement is implemented in this repository.

## Seasonal accounting

Seasonal rewards use the ecosystem allocation through a separately funded distributor. Reward budgets and payout rules do not create a continuous onchain emission or a claim on staking principal.

Index `Staked`, `Renewed`, `Redeemed`, `PositionRecovered` and `PositionReleased` events, including transaction/log order. Integrate each position's principal over the intersection of its active interval and the season interval. Maturity needs no transaction/event: close its active interval at `unlockAt`. A renewal opens a new active interval; partial renewal splits amount between the old matured ID and a new ID. Never use delegated votes or current active balance as a substitute for historical token-time.

Position ID remains stable on recovery. Apply the [rescue/enforcement points policy](#points-treatment-after-seizure): pending cases suspend accrual and payout, verified rescue preserves and migrates unpaid points plus otherwise eligible custody-period accrual, and confirmed enforcement cancels the affected account's entire unpaid program balance regardless of seizure size. Already paid tokens are not recalled by this process. Partial redemption/renewal must not duplicate already accrued rights: an indexer retains accrued pre-split rights on the old ID and credits the new ID only from the renewal event. Fully redeemed positions still have historical owner/payment records; deletion of live storage does not delete already accrued rights. A future distributor must commit the season's exact policy, prevent duplicate claims and apply case decisions between snapshot and payout. Snapshotting points before seizure must not leave a cancelled allocation claimable: payout authorization must consult current case status or use a distribution mechanism that can invalidate affected unpaid claims. Reconcile chain reorganizations and process each event/case adjustment idempotently. Retain cancellation and migration history rather than deleting the original accrual records. No funded distributor or final season economics are implemented here.

Required points-integration acceptance cases (not implemented token tests): partial liquid seizure cancels all unpaid program points only after confirmed enforcement; rescue never cancels the victim's points; rescue backfill ends at original maturity; multiple seizures do not cancel or migrate twice; unrelated accounts remain unaffected; a case opened after a season snapshot blocks stale payout; token release alone does not reinstate enforcement eligibility; already paid rewards cannot be claimed again. The deployed points system must also demonstrate verified-recipient handling and the correction/reorganization paths before this policy is described as operational.

## Product fees and savings allowance

Required integration behavior: sSTRN supplies the shared active-stake percentage; each product independently sets its ordinary fees and enforces a cumulative savings allowance per beneficiary. Measure actual fees waived in the product fee asset, share the allowance across deposits and redemptions, and use fixed 30-day periods with no rollover. Product parameter authority schedules allowance amounts for the next period without clearing consumed usage. Different beneficiaries and products have independent allowances; this is not a shared first-come budget, a person-level limit or a protocol-wide ceiling. No central cross-product controller is required.

A read-only percentage cannot enforce cumulative spending. Each product must authenticate its beneficiary, consume allowance only on successful fee settlement, and consume nothing on previews, reverts or skipped queue requests. For queued product withdrawals, eligibility is checked for the current claim owner at successful settlement; expiry while pending may reduce the discount, and minimum-payout protections remain mandatory. At exhaustion, ordinary fees apply subject to user execution limits. These are external integration requirements; production allowance amounts, period activation timestamps, concrete queued-withdrawal integration remain outstanding product work.

Only the percentage getter and clearly labeled integration fixture are implemented here; no production cumulative cap enforcement is claimed. Actual sUSDat integration remains a separate upgrade/audit. Each product calibrates its own fees to cover execution costs after the maximum 25% discount; the percentage getter does not impose a cost floor. Product fee and allowance inputs are not STRN/sSTRN deployment parameters.

## Production integration inputs

Set product fees, allowance amounts and period activation timestamps from the product economics. Specify and verify the deployed governance strategy, season budgets and distribution rules, beneficiary authentication, case authority and rescue-recipient verification. These are external-system inputs, not STRN/sSTRN deployment parameters.
