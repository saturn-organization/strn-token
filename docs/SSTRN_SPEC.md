# sSTRN implementation specification

Status: engineering audit candidate; not independently audited or deployed. This specification describes the implemented contract and its configuration. Initial deployment duration is 120 days. STRN and sSTRN provide historical voting primitives; external governance, points and product-fee enforcement are described in [integration requirements](INTEGRATION_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Requirements

- One STRN backs one nontransferable sSTRN receipt; principal remains separate from rewards.
- Positions have exact timestamp maturities and require explicit owner renewal. Mature claims remain redeemable and retain voting units.
- Active stake supplies a reusable discount percentage: zero below 5,000 STRN, 1.25% at 5,000 and a maximum 25% at 100,000.
- Delegation changes voting authority only; it does not transfer principal, discounts or external points entitlement.
- Recovery preserves backing and maturity while disabling voting, renewal and discounts. A separate authorized release restores ordinary eligibility subject to contract restrictions.

## State and gas bounds

Use explicit positions with a globally unique ID, owner, principal and exact timestamp maturity. Each ordinary account can own at most 32 outstanding positions. Recovery-held positions are excluded from that enumeration and have no global position-count limit. This engineering bound makes active-balance lookup at most 32 reads independent of system size or lifetime history. No keeper is needed: a position is active iff `block.timestamp < unlockAt`. Timestamp rounding is forbidden. Fully redeemed positions leave the owner's bounded set; historical events remain indexable. There is no transaction scanning all stakers.

Compare alternatives: unbounded position arrays make discount queries unsafe; day buckets change precise maturities; custom balanced trees add substantial review surface; a timestamp trie requires many storage writes per stake. A bounded set is the simpler candidate. Only the owner can create or renew positions, preventing third-party slot/relocking grief. At 32 positions, a new stake requires fully redeeming an eligible matured position to free a slot; maturity alone does not remove a position. Recovery reuses each existing record: owner equal to the staking proxy identifies recovery custody, with its receipt held by that proxy and no entry in an ordinary owner array. Recovery and release address one ID; no recovery-array scan or per-seizure deployment is needed. Ordinary receipt release respects the recipient's 32-slot bound; mature underlying release needs no slot.

## External surface and lifecycle

- `initialize(admin, token, duration, adminDelay)`: transparent proxy, atomic initialization; implementation locked. No hardcoded production addresses or duration. Duration bounds 1 day–365 days; the initial deployment duration is 120 days (10,368,000 seconds), supplied explicitly through configuration. STRN is the immutable-by-policy underlying address in namespaced storage.
- `stake(amount) returns (id)`: self only, positive amount, available slot, unpaused, unrestricted caller/custody, registered protected STRN custody. Exact balance delta required; mint exactly the received principal. All amounts use 18-decimal token units.
- `redeem(id, amount)`: owner only, positive amount up to position principal, maturity reached. Burn receipt and return exactly that amount to owner. Partial redemption preserves remaining maturity. No third-party recipient or allowance redemption. Local staking pause does not block matured withdrawal; underlying STRN pause/blacklist still does, and failure rolls the entire operation back.
- `renew(id, amount) returns (newId)`: owner only, matured principal only, positive amount, unpaused and unrestricted. New maturity uses the current duration. Full renewal reuses the slot; partial renewal creates a new position and leaves the unrenewed remainder matured. No transfer, mint, compounding or automatic renewal. Existing maturities never change with parameter updates.
- `position(id)`, `positionIds(owner)`, `activeBalanceOf(owner)`, `maturedBalanceOf(owner)`, `principalLiability()`, `excessUnderlying()`: deterministic state/views. Ordinary receipt `balanceOf` equals active plus matured principal; the staking proxy's balance represents recovery-held principal, excluded from active/matured ordinary views. `totalSupply` equals principal liability including recovery.
- `getFeeDiscountBps(owner)`: public read-only, 0–2,500 basis points. Below 5,000 active tokens returns zero; otherwise `min(2500, floor(active * 2500 / 100000e18))`. Restricted owner/custody or either paused contract returns zero. Delegation does not redirect discounts. Quote percentage applies to the whole product fee: savings are rounded down, fee equals original fee minus savings. No cost floor.
- Standard ERC20 balance/metadata/events and ERC5805/6372 vote/delegation views. Ordinary receipt `transfer`, `transferFrom` and `approve` revert; no permits, public burn, marketplace or collateral mechanics are provided.

Use custom errors for invalid address/amount/duration, capacity, invalid owner/maturity, restriction, missing custody protection, transfer prohibition and receipt mismatch. Emit stake, redeem, renew and recovery events with IDs, owner, amount and maturities; emit parameter changes. No caller can write arbitrary points or reward entitlement.

## Voting

Use pinned OpenZeppelin ERC20Votes/EIP712 with timestamp clock and signature nonces. Self-delegate on the first receipt unless the owner has already explicitly selected a delegate (including opting out to zero). Delegation can change freely and moves voting authority only. Same-timestamp operations coalesce; historical reads require a past timestamp. Maturity and renewal never change votes; mint, redemption, seizure and release do. Recovery-held receipts cannot vote or be delegated. An owner may not delegate while blacklisted; a blacklisted destination may not be newly selected. Existing historical checkpoints are not rewritten by blacklist changes.

STRN supplies historical liquid delegated votes using the same timestamp clock. A platform strategy sums both contracts' getPastVotes at the same past timestamp; liquid backing units are already excluded. Do not add token supplies together or subtract backing twice from delegated votes. See SPEC.md for the full-supply denominator and recovery exclusions. A deployed platform strategy/Governor remains outside this repository.

## Controls and recovery

Separate `PAUSER_ROLE`, `UNPAUSER_ROLE`, `PARAMETER_MANAGER_ROLE`, `SEIZER_ROLE` and `RELEASER_ROLE`, administered by the reviewed five-day timelock. ProxyAdmin ownership also belongs to that timelock; default-admin transfer delay is distinct. Parameter manager changes future duration only. Inherit STRN blacklist policy instead of a second independently drifting blacklist.

`recoverPosition(id)` requires staking SEIZER_ROLE and a blacklisted ordinary position owner, protects current default-admin ownership, and moves the WHOLE existing position into contract-held recovery custody. Principal, exact unlock date and ID remain intact. Remove it from ordinary enumeration; hold the matching receipt on the staking proxy, which cannot delegate. No recovery-recipient wallet capacity or rotation is involved. Recovery works during pauses and even if the underlying custody is blacklisted; no STRN moves. Repeat seizure is rejected. Recovery-held positions cannot renew, give discounts, use ordinary redemption, or regain eligibility through maturity or changing STRN's recovery recipient.

`releasePosition(id, recipient, redeemUnderlying)` requires RELEASER_ROLE, separately assigned from SEIZER_ROLE. Releases are immediate authorized operations with no per-release timelock. Role administration remains under the five-day timelock. Require an existing recovery-held position and an unrestricted nonzero recipient other than the staking proxy or any protected staking custody. With `redeemUnderlying=false`, restore the same position to the recipient's bounded ordinary list, preserve ID/principal/maturity, and restore ordinary voting and discount eligibility. This is allowed before or after maturity. With `redeemUnderlying=true`, require original maturity, close the whole claim, burn the contract-held receipt, and transfer exact underlying principal directly to the approved recipient. There is no intermediate unrestricted withdrawal into a recovery wallet. Both paths are explicit authorized release; wallet ownership by itself conveys no release power. Local staking pause allows release; underlying restrictions still apply to underlying transfers, with atomic rollback. Receipt release checks recipient eligibility, while asset pause/custody blacklist continue blocking its withdrawals and discounts as usual.

Seizure removes current delegated weight without reducing total-supply checkpoints. `getPastTotalSupply` continues to include recovery-held liability; the governance platform excludes historical recovered principal when deriving its eligible-supply denominator, rather than changing token supply onchain. Separate `recoveredPrincipal()` and `getPastRecoveredPrincipal(timepoint)` expose current/historical recovery quantities. Past reads use the same timestamp clock as votes. Seizure/release does not rewrite an earlier proposal snapshot; cancellation or emergency disqualification would need a separately specified governance mechanism. Authorized underlying release ends the recovery restriction and credits liquid voting units under STRN's existing delegation; receipt release credits sSTRN units. Do not substitute sSTRN supply for a combined STRN/sSTRN denominator or double-count backing.

`recoverExcess(amount)` is timelock-admin only, sends only balance above receipt supply to the controlled unrestricted recovery recipient, and never borrows principal. No reward funding, reward liabilities, reward payout or emission schedule is held in this contract. Rewards must use a separate funded distributor; do not deposit reward reserves here. Reentrancy guard plus atomic rollback protects stake, redemption, renewal, recovery, release and excess recovery.

## External integrations

The percentage getter does not charge product fees or consume an allowance. Neither token stores points, pays rewards or deploys a governance strategy. See [external integration requirements](INTEGRATION_REQUIREMENTS.md) for beneficiary authentication, settlement-time eligibility, savings allowances, points recovery and combined governance.

## Verification and launch gates

Test backing/supply conservation, failures and allowance rollback, donations/excess, exact expiry boundaries, partial renewal/redemption, multiple positions/capacity, arbitrary inactivity, future duration changes, recovery with active/matured claims, pause/blacklist/custody interaction, role attacks, delegate signatures/nonces, historical votes and no backing double count. Include independent stateful reference accounting, fuzz boundaries, maximum-capacity gas measurements and adversarial tokens. Verify proxy initialization/compatible upgrades and reject a compiled incompatible layout fixture. Check both tokens against their committed ABI/storage and gas baselines. Generate evidence for the reviewed commit without committing execution output.

The implemented 32-position limit requires production capacity acceptance; changing it requires a source change and review. Complete deployment role/address inputs and independent audit review before launch. Product allowances, governance-platform integration and season/distribution policy remain external integration work.
